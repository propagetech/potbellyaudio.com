01

How these personas were built

Potbelly sells a service to other businesses, not a product to consumers, so the personas below are buyer personas: the person at a client organisation who finds the site, evaluates it, and decides whether to message.

Every persona is built from segments Pooja has already named herself — the "Who We Work With" section on the live page (Production Houses, OTT & Streaming, Animation Studios, Advertising Agencies, International Content Owners/Distributors, Brands & Corporate, E-learning, Gaming) and the "Refer Potbelly" list. Nothing here invents a new market or a new audience. That follows the same rule the July rebuild broke: positioning and audience come from the client's own words, not from outside research layered on top. This document only asks "given the buyers she already told us about, does the current page make it easy for each of them to say yes?"

02

The five buyer personas

Five recurring buyer types, drawn from the segments already on the page. Names are placeholders for readability, not real clients.

R
Reema, Localisation Lead
OTT / streaming platform

"I need one vendor who can take a season into six languages at once, without six different quality bars."

Goals

  • Consistent QC across every language in a release window
  • One accountable team instead of juggling freelancers per language
  • Predictable turnaround before a launch date

Objections

  • Can they really run several languages in parallel at scale, or is "Built for Volume" just a slogan?
  • What happens to schedule risk if one language's talent falls through?
Lands on: hero → "What We Do" grid → "Built for Volume" card. No proof of concurrent multi-language delivery at scale beyond the card's one line.
K
Karan, Broadcast Producer
Advertising / creative agency

"My client will want three tone options and a change after the client call. I need a partner who moves at agency speed, not studio speed."

Goals

  • Right voice, fast, with room for a same-day revision round
  • A single contact who can join a call with his client if needed

Objections

  • Is the talent pool right for brand-voice work, not just narration?
  • How many revision rounds are included before it costs extra?
Lands on: "Hear the Difference" catalog (Mahindra Scorpio spots are exactly his use case) but nothing states revision policy or typical timeline.
A
Anita, Post-Production Supervisor
Production house / OTT delivery

"Dubbing direction and sync accuracy make or break the delivery QC. I need to see they've handled long-form before."

Goals

  • End-to-end pipeline (script → cast → direct → record → mix → QC) under one roof
  • Confidence on broadcast/OTT delivery specs

Objections

  • Track record on long-form (feature/series), not just short ads and explainers
  • Does the site show the workflow, or just list service names?
Lands on: "What We Do" (all eight services listed, good) but the services read as a menu, not a pipeline — nothing shows how they connect start to finish.
V
Vikram, L&D / Brand Manager
Corporate, e-learning

"I've never bought dubbing before. I just need someone to tell me what to send and what it costs."

Goals

  • Low-friction first contact (WhatsApp, plain language)
  • Clear idea of process and rough cost before committing time

Objections

  • Is my project too small for a studio that also handles OTT-scale work?
  • No visible pricing signal or minimum project size anywhere on the page
Lands on: WhatsApp-first CTAs (his best-fit contact channel, already done well) but nothing reassures him a small corporate job is welcome.
S
Sofia, Content Acquisition Manager
International distributor / content owner

"The last localisation partner gave us something technically correct but it felt translated. And the content wasn't public yet — I needed to trust them with that."

Goals

  • Content that feels native in the target market, not literal
  • Confidence pre-release material stays confidential

Objections

  • Can one Mumbai studio really understand nuance across many international languages?
  • No confidentiality/NDA statement visible anywhere on the site
Lands on: "We Get the Original" card (speaks directly to her worry) and the Languages section, but nothing addresses confidentiality for unreleased content.

04

Room for improvement

Ranked by how many personas it unblocks. Each item is tagged by who it depends on — matching the same rule as the portfolio and About items already open with Pooja: we don't publish a claim, number, or client name she hasn't confirmed.

Turn the service list into a visible pipeline Ours

"What We Do" already lists all eight services in the right order (script/translation → casting → dubbing direction → recording & mixing → post-production → QC). A short "how a project moves through Potbelly" strip using those same eight names, just sequenced, would answer Anita's and Reema's biggest question without adding a single new claim.

Helps: Anita, Reema, Vikram

Self-select entry points by buyer type Ours

"Who We Work With" already lists all eight segments. Turning those into anchor links from the hero (or a thin strip right under it) lets a visitor confirm "this is for people like me" in the first three seconds, instead of scrolling to find themselves further down the page.

Helps: all five

Group the existing "Hear the Difference" catalog by use case Ours

The seven videos already on the page cover automotive (Mahindra Scorpio), kids/education (Kidzania), and CSR (Flynote) — exactly Karan's and Vikram's categories. A label or filter over the existing grid (no new videos needed) helps each persona find their own use case faster.

Helps: Karan, Vikram, Anita

State turnaround and revision expectations Needs Pooja

Karan and Vikram both hesitate without a rough sense of "how many revision rounds" and "how fast." We can't publish a number we haven't been given — this needs her actual policy, even approximate (e.g. "two rounds included," "typical turnaround for a 3-minute spot is X days").

Helps: Karan, Vikram

Add a confidentiality / NDA line Needs Pooja

Sofia's segment (international distributors, pre-release content) explicitly worries about leaks before launch. Even one sentence ("NDA on request for unreleased content") would remove an objection the current page never addresses — but it needs her to confirm that's actually her practice.

Helps: Sofia, Anita

Proof behind "Built for Volume" and case studies generally Needs Pooja, already open

This is the same open item already logged from the portfolio conversation: she said yes to updating the portfolio and adding reels in general, but hasn't confirmed the named examples (Kushals Jewellery, Thermax & Shellmax, road safety campaign) one by one, or supplied reel links/logos. Reema and Anita are the two personas who most need this proof to trust the scale claims. No new ask here — just flagging it's the same blocker, seen from the persona side this time.

Helps: Reema, Anita, Karan

Founder credibility for About Needs Pooja, already open

Also already open: About has never had a photo of Pooja, and the copy is two short lines. Sofia and Anita both weigh "who is actually behind this" before trusting a smaller studio with international or long-form work. A fuller founder bio and a real photo (not just the CD illustration) would help — but needs her input on what to include.

Helps: Sofia, Anita

Nothing to add: language coverage objection is already handled Already solved

Sofia's "do they cover my specific language" worry is already answered by the existing "Don't see your language? Ask Us" WhatsApp CTA at the end of the Languages section. No change needed there — noted so it isn't mistaken for a gap.
