*Potbelly Audio — Photo Shot List*

Purpose: replace stock photography on the site with real photos of the studio and team, so the site matches "human-led" and stops reading as generic/foreign. Every one of these is shootable on a phone.

*HOW TO SHOOT THESE (read once, applies to every card below)*

_Camera / phone settings_
- Shoot landscape orientation (all cards are wide rectangles, not square or portrait)
- Turn the phone's grid lines ON (Settings → Camera → Grid) — use it to keep verticals straight
- Tap the subject to focus, then hold and drag down slightly to underexpose a touch — the site's palette is warm and dark, so slightly moodier beats bright and flat
- Turn HDR off if your phone forces a flat, over-lit look with it on
- Never use flash — flattens the image, fastest way to make a real photo look fake
- Portrait Mode (blur) off unless a card below says otherwise
- Shoot each setup 5-6 times from slightly different angles/moments, pick the best after

_Light_
- Prefer whatever light is already in the room: window daylight, or the studio's own warm practical lamps
- Avoid cold white/blue LED panel light and greenish tube-light — clashes with the site's warm palette (terracotta, warm cream, warm near-black) and reads "corporate stock photo"
- If adding light, use a warm desk lamp ("warm white" bulb setting, not "daylight"/"cool white") from the side or slightly above eye level
- Console/gear screens glowing in a dim room look great — keep that if the studio already looks like that at night

_People_
- Nobody looks at or smiles at the camera — shoot mid-task, mid-conversation, mid-listen, candid not posed
- Wardrobe: whatever people would normally wear to a session, no need to dress up

_Sending files_
- Send full-resolution originals, not screenshots
- On WhatsApp, attach as Document, not Photo/Gallery — Photo send compresses and it'll show
- If there are many, a shared Google Drive folder is easier
- No filters, no Instagram presets — send straight out of the camera
