*SECTION 2 — Why Potbelly? (6 cards)*

*10. Experience*
Shot: a senior engineer or Pooja at a console/desk they clearly know well, hands confidently on the gear, mid-adjustment, not posed
Angle: 3/4 angle at the console, waist-up
Light: warm, console-glow style, matching cards 5-7

*11. The Right People*
Shot: a genuine group shot — two or three team members together in conversation or working side by side, not a lineup, not everyone facing camera
Angle: candid, slightly wide, mid-interaction
Light: natural room light is fine here

*12. Scale*
Shot: an establishing shot that reads "more than one studio" — three studio doors down a corridor, or a wide shot of one full studio (booth + control room together)
Angle: wide, from a fixed point that shows depth
Light: natural

*13. One Point of Contact*
Shot: someone (Pooja or a coordinator) on a call at a laptop, headset on, clearly coordinating
Angle: 3/4 angle at the desk, laptop and headset visible
Light: warm desk-lamp light, same family as Translation

*14. We Get the Original*
Shot: hands referencing an original script/video still next to the localised draft — a monitor showing a paused video frame next to a printed or on-screen script works well
Angle: top-down or steep 3/4 over the desk, similar to Translation
Light: natural desk light

*15. Quality Matters*
Shot: different from Quality Control (card 8) — the checking artifact itself: a marked-up QC sheet with visible tick marks/corrections, headphones resting beside it (not worn), pen mid-mark
Angle: top-down close-up on the sheet and pen, headphones at the edge of frame
Light: natural desk light
