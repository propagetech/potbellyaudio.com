## Part 2: the full list, by priority

### Blocking, nothing ships without these

1. **WhatsApp Business number.** Which number should every WhatsApp link on the site open? It has to
   be WhatsApp enabled, a landline will not work. Should it be a personal number or a business
   account?
2. **Work we can publish.** The old site has seven YouTube projects (Mahindra Scorpio x2, Kidzania
   x4, Flynote). Are these still fine to show? What else can we add, and are there projects from the
   last two or three years we should lead with instead?
3. **Client names.** Which clients can we name, and which logos can we display? If permission is
   unclear for any of them, we describe the work without the brand name.
4. **The claims we should not guess at.** Three studios, correct? How many years should we say?
   Roughly how many people are on the team, or is it a core team plus a network?

### Important, changes what the page says

5. **Your own credits.** Public sources credit you as the Hindi voice of Jennifer Lawrence in
   Hunger Games and X-Men, Bheem in the English dub of Chhota Bheem, and work on Smurfs 2 and The
   Croods. That is the strongest trust signal this business has and the old site did not use it at
   all. Can we publish a short, accurate version, and which credits do you want named?
6. **Audio samples.** Even a few voice samples per language. This is the one asset that would change
   the site the most.
7. **Studio and team photos.** Real photos of the rooms, a session in progress, the team. We will
   not use stock photos of somebody else's studio.
8. **Languages.** Your list ends with "and more". Which international languages have you actually
   delivered? We only want to list what is real.
9. **Turnaround.** What should we say when someone asks how fast you are? Even a range helps, and it
   is the second question every buyer asks.
10. **Social links.** We have Facebook, Twitter and Instagram from the old site. Your footer lists
    Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and Facebook. Can you send the LinkedIn and YouTube URLs, and
    should Twitter stay or go?

### Strategic, worth ten minutes of your time

11. **AI dubbing.** Buyers ask about this constantly in 2026. Do you use AI anywhere in the
    pipeline, and what is your position? Either answer is fine, but the site should say something,
    because saying nothing reads as hiding.
12. **Security and NDAs.** OTT and studio clients ask how content is handled before delivery. Is
    there a process worth stating?
13. **Japan and Korea.** The old version of the site claimed expertise there. Was there any real
    work behind that? If yes it becomes a line in the languages section. If no we remove it entirely.
14. **Referrals.** Your referral section is unusual and we like it. Is there anything you want to
    offer a referrer, or is it purely a goodwill ask?
15. **Priority segment.** Of the eight audiences you listed, which one do you most want more of next
    year? It will not change the page, but it will change which section comes first inside the work
    and proof blocks.
