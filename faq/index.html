Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about dubbing, localization, and audio production.

Turnaround & Pricing

How quickly can you deliver a dubbing project?

Standard turnaround is 7-14 days depending on project scope and language. We also offer express (3-7 days) and rush (1-2 days) services for additional fees. Contact us with your timeline and we'll confirm availability.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on project scope: content duration, language, number of characters, turnaround time, and revision allowance. We provide custom quotes after understanding your specific requirements. Request a free consultation for an estimate.

Do you offer rush or emergency turnarounds?

Yes, we offer rush services (1-2 days) for select projects. Availability depends on current workload. Contact us immediately with your deadline and we'll confirm if we can accommodate.

Services & Languages

What languages do you offer?

Primary: Japanese, Korean. Secondary: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Bengali, Marathi, English. We also work with other languages on request and can handle international projects.

What's the difference between dubbing and voice-over?

Dubbing replaces the original dialogue with new voice in another language, synced to the actor's lip movement. Voice-over is a narrator or character voice layered over the original audio. Dubbing requires more precise timing and cultural adaptation.

Can you handle Japanese and Korean anime dubbing?

Yes, anime dubbing is a specialty. We understand anime conventions, character archetypes, and cultural nuances for both Japanese and Korean markets. We work with experienced anime voice directors and actors.

Do you provide voice actors or do I supply them?

We handle everything: casting, voice direction, and recording. We maintain a large pool of professional voice talent across all languages. You can also provide preferred voice actors if needed.

Quality & Delivery

What formats do you deliver in?

We deliver in industry-standard formats: MP3, WAV, AAC, and format-specific for streaming platforms (Netflix, YouTube, etc.). We can also provide stems, mixed tracks, or complete deliverables based on your requirements.

What is your quality guarantee?

Every project is personally reviewed by founder Pooja Punjabi for artistic and technical excellence. We provide unlimited revisions within the project scope to ensure you're satisfied. We only deliver broadcast and OTT-ready quality.

Do you work with streaming platforms like Netflix?

Yes, we have experience with major streaming platforms' technical requirements and quality standards. We deliver OTT-compliant audio meeting Netflix, Amazon Prime, Coupang, and other platform specifications.
