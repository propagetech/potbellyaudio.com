01 / Script & language

Get the words right first.

A weak script cannot be rescued by a strong actor. Everything upstream of the booth happens here: translation, adaptation for the audience actually watching, and a full round of quality control before a single line is recorded.

Talk about a script

A

Translation & Localisation

Translation that considers context, culture, tone and audience, not just words. The test is whether a native speaker forgets they are watching a dub.

B

Script-level QC

Every script clears quality control before recording starts. A problem caught on the page costs a line. The same problem caught after the mix costs the session.

Twenty-one languages, and counting

02 / Voice & performance

The performance is the product.

Localisation should make content feel native, not make it feel like a translation. That is a casting decision and a direction decision long before it is a technical one, which is why a director sits in on the session and not just an engineer.

Brief us on a project

A

Casting

A wide pool of voice talent across languages, age groups and performance styles, with options sent back so you can hear the choice, not just read it.

B

Dubbing direction

Experienced dubbing directors who know how to get the right performance from every artist, take after take, across a full season.

C

Dubbing

Natural, well directed dubbing that keeps the performance, emotion and intent of the original, in sync and in character.

D

Voiceovers

The right voice for films, documentaries, advertising, corporate and digital content, briefed properly and delivered ready to cut.
