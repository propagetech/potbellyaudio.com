About Potbelly Audio

Founded 2013. Founder-led. Artist-driven. Quality-obsessed.

Our Founder: Pooja Punjabi

Potbelly Audio was founded by Pooja Punjabi, a professional voice-over artist and dubbing director with expertise in multiple languages and over 15 years in the audio industry.

Pooja's background as a creative professional gives Potbelly Audio a unique advantage: we understand both the artistic vision and the technical demands of high-quality audio production. Her personal involvement in every project ensures consistency and excellence.

Key Milestones

  • 2009: Pooja enters the audio industry as a professional voice-over artist
  • 2013: Potbelly Audio founded to serve growing demand for quality dubbing and localization
  • 2015+: Expansion into Japanese and Korean markets, anime dubbing specialization
  • Today: Serving production companies, OTT platforms, and creative agencies across Japan, Korea, and India

Our Process

Every project follows a structured workflow to ensure quality, consistency, and on-time delivery:

1. Intake & Planning

We understand your content, target audience, cultural nuances, and delivery requirements. Custom scope for every project.

2. Casting & Direction

Select the right voice talent. Detailed creative direction ensures the performance matches your vision.

3. Recording & Sync

Professional studio recording with careful timing and lip-sync verification. Multiple takes available.

4. Quality Control

Technical review, audio processing, and verification against original. Nothing ships without our seal.

5. Revision & Polish

Your feedback integrated. Re-records if needed. Final tweaks for perfection.

6. Delivery

Handoff in your required format. Support available for technical implementation.

Our Commitment

  • Quality First: Every project is personally reviewed by Pooja Punjabi for artistic and technical excellence.
  • Cultural Authenticity: We don't just translate; we transcreate. Deep understanding of Japanese, Korean, and Indian cultural nuances.
  • Fast Turnarounds: Agile process and experienced team mean quick delivery without sacrificing quality.
  • Transparent Communication: Regular updates, responsive to feedback, collaborative partnership approach.
  • Technical Excellence: Broadcast and OTT-ready quality. Professional equipment, standards compliance, format flexibility.
